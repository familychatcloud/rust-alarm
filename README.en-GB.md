A wake-up call with a useful message. Noler speaks your alarm label and keeps everyday alarms offline. No adverts, accounts, cloud syncing or subscriptions.

For the school run, an early shift or a train to catch, wake up to a useful message. Noler is a lightweight offline talking alarm clock that reads your alarm label aloud with on-device speech.

Set a one-off alarm or repeat it on selected days of the week. Check your next alarm at a glance and use large snooze and dismiss controls. An alarm sound acts as a fallback when speech cannot be used.

Normal alarm use needs no Wi-Fi or mobile data. Scheduling, labels, speech, alarm records, settings and attachments remain on your device. No adverts, analytics, behavioural tracking, account, cloud sync, subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Battery saving starts with fewer tasks. Weather and horoscope feeds, advertising, analytics, cloud syncing and background online feeds are absent, so Noler avoids their network and processing workload. Actual battery use varies by device, settings and alarms; no fixed saving is guaranteed.

Built with Rust and Dioxus for a shared application core and interface, with 39 interface locales. No sleep tracking, snore recording or news feeds are bundled into your alarm clock.

Android includes an English voice from the open-source Flite engine as a fallback if no usable system text-to-speech engine is available. Voices depend on installed languages. Optional keep-alive mode is off by default and may use more battery.

Live pages such as Terms and Privacy connect only when you choose to open them in Settings.
